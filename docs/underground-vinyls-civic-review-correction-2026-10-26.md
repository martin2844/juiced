# Civic visual review correction

The user's Android report invalidates our earlier acceptance of the Civic vinyls.
Reinspection of all three stored native views of each of its ten accepted options
confirms that the B threshold was too permissive. All ten are now C in the source
review manifest, with individual reasons and their previous decisions retained
in the amendment history. The local full gallery has been regenerated.

Most unambiguous error: both `yourmom` designs have their first text line clipped
below the window in both side views, despite the original review calling the text
contained and legible. Hood/front distortion and panel discontinuities affect
several other designs. The electrical design also fails the requested legibility
threshold in the existing captures.

The importer projects an NFS atlas onto a stock Juiced paint shell using triangle
normals and whole-side coordinate bounds. Its artwork fitting does not constrain
important text or graphics to the actual paintable region below the windows.
Changing a tier does not repair that geometry or texture placement. These assets
need revised placement and fresh native captures before acceptance.

Counts after this limited correction: 8 A, 89 B, 125 C, 19 D. The other cars have
not been revalidated by this correction. The previously published 107-option ZIP
and the installed Android package remain unchanged; this is an evaluation change,
not a replacement release. No save or equipped-vinyl index has been changed.

Local evidence: `/tmp/honda-review-0.png`, `/tmp/honda-review-5.png`, assembled
from `out/vinyl-research/v2-reviewed-shots/CIVIC_97_DECAL_NFS_*_{0,1,2}.bmp`.
